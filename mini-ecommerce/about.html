OUR STORY

A modern mini e-commerce brand

NovaShop is a demo company created to present a complete front-end e-commerce flow using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript. The store focuses on useful products across electronics, fashion, home and beauty.

The experience is designed around clear navigation, dynamic products, easy cart management and a simple administration dashboard.
